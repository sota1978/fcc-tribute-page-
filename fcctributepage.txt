                                                                                                                      <h2>Biography</h2>
                                                                                                                              <p>
                                                                                                                                        Abdulmanap Nurmagomedov (1962–2020) was a legendary Russian mixed martial arts coach, widely regarded for his discipline and philosophy in combat sports. A former athlete and army veteran, he trained hundreds of fighters—most famously his son, Khabib Nurmagomedov, the undefeated UFC lightweight champion.
                                                                                                                                                </p>
                                                                                                                                                        <p>
                                                                                                                                                                  He held a degree in physical education and developed one of Dagestan's most respected training systems. His coaching career emphasized humility, discipline, and national pride. His influence extended beyond MMA, earning recognition from top athletes and officials across Russia and the world.
                                                                                                                                                                          </p>
                                                                                                                                                                                  <p>
                                                                                                                                                                                            Abdulmanap passed away in 2020 due to complications related to COVID-19, leaving behind a powerful legacy in the martial arts world.
                                                                                                                                                                                                    </p>

                                                                                                                                                                                                            <h3>Key Achievements & Milestones</h3>
                                                                                                                                                                                                                    <ul>
                                                                                                                                                                                                                              <li><strong>1987:</strong> Graduated with a degree in Physical Education.</li>
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        <li><strong>1990s:</strong> Started coaching wrestling and judo in Dagestan.</li>
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  <li><strong>2001:</strong> Trained his son Khabib from a young age in freestyle wrestling, sambo, and judo.</li>
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            <li><strong>2012:</strong> Became an Honored Coach of Russia.</li>
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      <li><strong>2018:</strong> Coached Khabib to win the UFC Lightweight Championship.</li>
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                <li><strong>2020:</strong> Passed away due to COVID-19 complications. Honored by global MMA community.</li>
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        </ul>
